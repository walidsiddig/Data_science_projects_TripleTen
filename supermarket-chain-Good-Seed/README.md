# Good Seed Supermarket – Sales Analysis

## Overview
Analyzed customer purchase behavior to help the supermarket chain "Good Seed" optimize promotions, inventory, and product recommendations.

## Data
- Transaction-level purchase data.  
- Customer demographics and product categories.  
- Target: Insights on customer segments and demand patterns.  

## Approach
1. **Data Preparation** – cleaned transaction logs, handled duplicates.  
2. **EDA** – sales trends, top products, seasonal effects.  
3. **Modeling** – clustering customers for segmentation.  
4. **Evaluation** – interpretation of clusters and purchase patterns.  

## Results
- Identified profitable customer segments.  
- Recommendations for marketing and stock planning.
