# Evaluate Taxi Company Performance

## Description
This project analyzes and compares the performance of multiple taxi companies using trip and revenue data. The insights help identify the most efficient providers and support business decisions.

## Dataset
The dataset includes taxi trip details such as company name, trip counts, and revenue over time.

## Methodology
- **EDA**: Compared performance across companies using summary statistics and visualizations.  
- **Feature Engineering**: Extracted relevant features for performance measurement.  
- **Analysis**: Assessed profitability, reliability, and market share among providers.  

## Results
- Highlighted top-performing companies.  
- Provided actionable recommendations for business strategy and partnerships.
